## Biography

I am an evolutionary ecologist and herpetologist. I completed my Master's degree at **Nanjing Forestry University**, where I conducted extensive research at the **Laboratory of Herpetology and Applied Conservation**. My academic journey is driven by a deep fascination with how ectothermic vertebrates survive and adapt in human-dominated landscapes.

Currently, I collaborate with local and international researchers to assess the impacts of coastal urbanization on amphibian physiology, monitor invasive reptiles, and propose realistic conservation policies to government bodies in South Korea.

## Research Interests

* **Ecological Adaptation:** Understanding physiological changes (e.g., salinity tolerance in tree frogs) in response to environmental shifts.
* **Conservation Biology & Biosecurity:** Developing mitigation strategies for endangered species like *Dryophytes suweonensis*.
* **Invasive Species Management:** Monitoring illegal pet trades, pathobiology, and establishing containment protocols for non-native species.
