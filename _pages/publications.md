Peer-reviewed journal articles, natural history notes, ecological studies, and conservation policy publications by **Kyongman Heo**.

**Kyongman Heo** is highlighted in bold. Authors are listed in the original published order. For publications with extensive author lists, authors following Kyongman Heo are abbreviated as *et al.* Equal-contribution authorship is indicated where applicable.

---

## 📄 Peer-Reviewed Journal Articles

### 2026

* **[Ocean Romance: Japanese Treefrogs Exploit Coastal Pools for Breeding Under Salinity and Habitat Constraints](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.73744)**
  **Kyongman Heo†**, Deyatima Ghosh†, Kevin R. Messenger, and Siti N. Othman
  *Ecology and Evolution* (2026)
  *Examines how Japanese treefrogs use coastal pools for reproduction and how salinity, freshwater availability, vegetation, and habitat structure affect breeding-site use.*
  **† These authors contributed equally to this study.**

---

### 2024

* **[Tracing the Geographic Origin of a Nonnative Red-Banded Snake (Colubridae: *Lycodon rufozonatus*) Found on Jeju Island, Republic of Korea](https://doi.org/10.1655/Herpetologica-D-23-00014)**
  Yucheol Shin†, **Kyongman Heo†**, Siti N. Othman, Yikweon Jang, Mi-Sook Min, and Amaël Borzée
  *Herpetologica* 80(1): 30–39 (2024)
  *Uses phylogenetic analysis to trace the geographic origin of a nonnative red-banded snake found on Jeju Island and evaluates its potential establishment through ecological niche modeling.*
  **† Yucheol Shin and Kyongman Heo contributed equally to this study.**

* **[Variation in Functional Connectivity Between Metapopulations in Urbanized and Forested Areas in an Endangered Salamander](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11252-023-01434-9)**
  Jong Yoon Jeon, Daecheol Jeong, Amaël Borzée, **Kyongman Heo**, *et al.*
  *Urban Ecosystems* 27: 111–124 (2024)
  *Compares functional connectivity among endangered salamander metapopulations in urbanized and forested landscapes and examines the effects of habitat fragmentation on gene flow.*

---

### 2019

* **[First Report of *Dryophytes japonicus* Tadpoles in Saline Environment](https://doi.org/10.30906/1026-2296-2019-26-2-87-90)**
  **Kyongman Heo**, Ye Inn Kim, Yoonhyuk Bae, Yikweon Jang, and Amaël Borzée
  *Russian Journal of Herpetology* 26(2): 87–90 (2019)
  *Reports Japanese treefrog tadpoles developing in saline coastal pools on Jeju Island and provides evidence of salinity tolerance in a Korean amphibian.*

---

### 2018

* **[Relationship Between Agro-Environmental Variables and Breeding Hylids in Rice Paddies](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-26222-w)**
  Amaël Borzée, **Kyongman Heo**, and Yikweon Jang
  *Scientific Reports* 8: 8049 (2018)
  *Examines how rice cultivation, water conditions, temperature, vegetation, and surrounding landscape variables influence the breeding activity of Japanese and Suweon treefrogs.*

---

### 2017

* **[Phylogeographic and Population Insights of the Asian Common Toad (*Bufo gargarizans*) in Korea and China: Population Isolation and Expansions as Response to the Ice Ages](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4044)**
  Amaël Borzée, Joana L. Santos, Santiago Sánchez-Ramírez, Yoonhyuk Bae, **Kyongman Heo**, *et al.*
  *PeerJ* 5: e4044 (2017)
  *Reconstructs the phylogeographic and demographic history of Asian common toad populations in Korea and China, including population isolation and expansion associated with past glacial periods.*

* **[Impact of Land Reclamation and Agricultural Water Regime on the Distribution and Conservation Status of the Endangered *Dryophytes suweonensis*](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.3872)**
  Amaël Borzée, Kyungmin Kim, **Kyongman Heo**, Piotr G. Jablonski, and Yikweon Jang
  *PeerJ* 5: e3872 (2017)
  *Evaluates the distribution and conservation status of the endangered Suweon treefrog and examines how land reclamation and agricultural water management affect its remaining habitat.*

---

### 2015

* **[Seoul, Keep Your Paddies! Implications for the Conservation of Hylid Species](https://doi.org/10.5635/ASED.2015.31.3.176)**
  Amaël Borzée, Jaeha Ahn, Sanha Kim, **Kyongman Heo**, and Yikweon Jang
  *Animal Systematics, Evolution and Diversity* 31(3): 176–181 (2015)
  *Highlights the importance of rice paddies and isolated wetland habitats within metropolitan Seoul for the conservation of native hylid treefrogs.*

## 📋 Conservation & Policy Articles

### 2024
